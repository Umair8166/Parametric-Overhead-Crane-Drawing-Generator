## 1. **Who is the user, and what do they need?**  
    - I assume that the user will be the sales person or a engineering team member that want to prepare an early quotation or preview (not the final structure) for answering the potential leads (customers) queries.  
    - They need to enter the details and checks basic requirements like will the crane fit the building, and save or reopens designs instead of create them every time.


## 2. **What did you assume (crane type, inputs, units, ranges), and why?**
    - I choose the single-girder EOT crane for this becuase it matches the reference drawing. I make the input fields (name, capacity, span, rail height and lifting height) as required and the other two input fields(building heigth and wall clearnace) as optional fields. I used the whole mm and tonnes units based on the reference drawing. And all the ranges like span(5000 - 30000mm), rail height (3000 - 15000 mm) etc. are completely assumed based on sanity limits.
  

## 3. **Where did each formula or rule come from, and how sure are you?**
    - The formulas and rules I have used in my project comes completely from the reference drawing in the assignment, based on the dimensions provided in the drawing. So, I can't say that I'm completely sure, because I haven't check the accuracy of the formulas or what formulas are actualy correct in crane designing principles.


## 4. **What did you leave out on purpose?**

    - I avoid the structure checks, like runway size, speed, wheel load calculation etc. 
    - I also avoid the other crane types, and multi-user features to keep our project simple and straigt forward.
